Common questions about AI consulting
and workflow design.

What can you help me automate?+

I can help design workflows for recurring tasks such as organizing incoming inquiries, preparing research summaries, and turning meeting notes into action lists. We start with your process, identify useful automation, and decide where human review belongs.

Can you help me choose an AI tool?+

Yes. I can advise you on AI use and help you compare tools from OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), and Google against your tasks, budget, and data requirements. The starting point is what you need to accomplish.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. We can start with the tools you already use. I know n8n for building workflows, alongside Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript when custom logic or an interface is useful.

Will AI do everything automatically?+

Not every task should be automated. We can build in checkpoints for reviewing AI output and agreeing what happens next, especially before information is shared or important actions are taken.

How does a project start?+

Start with one task you repeat, the tools involved, and the outcome you want. The workflow explorer helps you prepare a short project brief for that conversation.
